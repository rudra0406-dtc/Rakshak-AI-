## 1. Product Overview

Rakshak AI is an intelligent cybersecurity and digital fraud prevention ecosystem designed to protect individuals, families, professionals and organizations from evolving cyber threats.

The platform aims to combine artificial intelligence, machine learning, threat intelligence, security automation and human-guided incident response into one unified security environment.

Its purpose is not limited to identifying suspicious activities. It aims to help prevent fraud, detect threats, explain risks, guide victims and protect its own infrastructure.

Rakshak AI will be developed as a modular, scalable and security-focused software product.

## 2. Vision Statement

To build a trusted and intelligent digital defense ecosystem that makes cybersecurity more accessible, proactive and effective for everyone.

## 3. Mission Statement

To reduce the impact of digital fraud and cybercrime through intelligent threat detection, preventive security technologies, accessible assistance and continuously improving defensive systems.

## 4. Problem Statement

Digital fraud and cyber threats are becoming increasingly sophisticated.

Individuals and organizations face problems including:

- Phishing websites and malicious links.
- UPI and payment-related fraud.
- Fake banking and customer support communications.
- Identity theft and account takeover.
- Social engineering and impersonation.
- Fake employment and investment schemes.
- Malicious documents and files.
- Deepfake-assisted impersonation.
- Inadequate awareness of cybersecurity.
- Difficulty responding to cyber incidents.

Existing security solutions often address individual problems separately.

Users may struggle to understand whether something is fraudulent, why it is dangerous and what action they should take.

Rakshak AI aims to bring multiple defensive capabilities together into a unified platform.

## 5. Target Users

1. Individuals and everyday internet users.
2. Students and young professionals.
3. Families and senior citizens.
4. Small businesses.
5. Organizations and security teams.

The initial product focus will be individual users in India, with an architecture designed to support future expansion.

## 6. Core Product Pillars

### 6.1 Fraud Prevention
Identify suspicious links, messages, emails, QR codes, websites and other potential fraud indicators.

### 6.2 Threat Detection
Combine detection rules, machine learning, threat intelligence and evidence analysis to identify suspicious activity.

### 6.3 AI Intelligence
Provide an intelligent assistant capable of explaining threats, interpreting evidence and guiding users through security decisions.

### 6.4 Personal Cyber Protection
Provide account security guidance, identity protection, browser safety and consent-based family protection.

### 6.5 Incident Response
Help users organize evidence, understand immediate safety steps and navigate official reporting and recovery processes.

### 6.6 Platform Self-Defense
Protect Rakshak AI's own application, infrastructure, APIs, databases, AI systems and sensitive information.

### 6.7 Threat Intelligence
Collect, validate, correlate and maintain reliable information about emerging cyber threats.

### 6.8 Enterprise Security
Provide future organization-level dashboards, team management, security reporting and integrations.

## 7. Core Product Principles

- Security by design.
- Privacy by design.
- Evidence-based detection.
- Explainable AI decisions.
- Least-privilege access.
- Human approval for high-impact actions.
- Secure data handling.
- Continuous testing and improvement.
- Transparent limitations.
- Accessible user experience.

## 8. Product Boundaries

Rakshak AI is a defensive cybersecurity product.

It will not:

- Perform unauthorized access to third-party systems.
- Collect banking PINs, passwords or OTPs.
- Execute financial transactions.
- Monitor private communications without authorization.
- Perform destructive actions against external systems.
- Present uncertain AI predictions as confirmed facts.
- Claim absolute protection against every cyberattack.

All integrations and data collection must follow applicable permissions, privacy requirements and legal obligations.

## 9. Technical Direction

The proposed primary technology stack includes:

- Python and FastAPI for backend development.
- React and TypeScript for frontend development.
- PostgreSQL for structured data.
- Redis for future caching and background processing.
- Docker for service isolation.
- Git and GitHub for version control.
- Automated testing and secure CI/CD.

The initial architecture will follow a modular monolith approach, with selective service separation as the product grows.

## 10. Security Objectives

Rakshak AI must establish measurable security objectives:

1. Protect user identities and sensitive information.
2. Prevent unauthorized access.
3. Secure APIs and application components.
4. Isolate sensitive services and processing environments.
5. Protect AI models and tool integrations.
6. Monitor security events.
7. Maintain reliable backups and recovery procedures.
8. Continuously identify and resolve vulnerabilities.
9. Maintain auditable security decisions.
10. Validate production readiness through independent security testing.

## 11. Long-Term Objectives

- Build a comprehensive fraud intelligence platform.
- Develop specialized AI security agents.
- Introduce multilingual scam detection.
- Build reliable threat correlation capabilities.
- Develop browser and device protection integrations.
- Establish an incident response ecosystem.
- Expand into enterprise cybersecurity.
- Develop measurable and independently verifiable security capabilities.

## 12. Success Measurements

Product success will be evaluated through:

- Detection precision and recall.
- False-positive and false-negative rates.
- Time required to identify threats.
- Quality of evidence and explanations.
- User comprehension and satisfaction.
- Successful incident assistance workflows.
- Security vulnerabilities discovered and resolved.
- Service availability and recovery performance.
- Privacy and security audit results.

No accuracy or security performance claim will be published without supporting evaluation evidence.

## 13. Development Philosophy

Rakshak AI will follow a quality-first hybrid development approach.

The development process will include:

1. Research and requirements.
2. Architecture and technical documentation.
3. Incremental implementation.
4. Automated and manual testing.
5. Security verification.
6. Documentation and review.
7. Controlled deployment.
8. Continuous improvement.

Each major module must satisfy defined acceptance criteria before being considered complete.

## 14. Initial Development Scope

The first implementation milestones will prioritize:

- Secure project foundation.
- Authentication and authorization.
- Core backend architecture.
- URL analysis.
- Message analysis.
- Email analysis.
- QR code analysis.
- Evidence-based risk assessment.
- Initial AI assistant.
- Security monitoring.

Advanced capabilities will be introduced after the underlying foundations are validated.

## 15. Product Commitment

Rakshak AI is intended to become a long-term cybersecurity product.

Its development will prioritize real-world usefulness, engineering quality, security, privacy and responsible artificial intelligence.

The project will not measure success by the number of features alone, but by the reliability and effectiveness of the protection it provides.
