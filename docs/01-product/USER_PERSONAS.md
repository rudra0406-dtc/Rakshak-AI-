## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose

This document defines the primary user personas of Rakshak AI. It describes the different categories of people and organizations that may use the platform, their cybersecurity challenges, goals, expectations and requirements.

These personas will guide product design, feature prioritization, user experience decisions and future development.

### 1.2 Why User Personas Matter

Cybersecurity requirements differ between individuals, families, security professionals and organizations.

A student may need a simple way to check a suspicious message, while a security analyst may need detailed evidence and investigation tools.

Understanding these differences will help Rakshak AI provide relevant and accessible security capabilities.

## 2. Primary User Personas

### Persona 1: Individual Digital User

**Persona ID:** RP-001  
**User Category:** Individual  
**Technical Knowledge:** Basic to Intermediate

**Profile:**

An everyday internet user who regularly uses smartphones, messaging applications, online shopping, digital payments and social media.

**Goals:**
- Identify suspicious links and messages.
- Avoid online scams and financial fraud.
- Understand digital security warnings.
- Improve personal cybersecurity awareness.

**Challenges:**
- Difficulty identifying fake websites.
- Receiving fraudulent SMS and messaging content.
- Lack of technical knowledge about cyber threats.
- Confusion about what to do after encountering a suspicious message.

**Expected Rakshak AI Features:**
- URL Scanner.
- Message Scanner.
- Website Intelligence.
- AI Cybersecurity Assistant.
- Personal Security Dashboard.

**Success Scenario:**

The user receives a suspicious payment-related message, submits its content to Rakshak AI and receives an understandable risk assessment with supporting indicators and recommended precautions.

---

### Persona 2: Family Safety User

**Persona ID:** RP-002  
**User Category:** Family  
**Technical Knowledge:** Basic

**Profile:**

A person who wants to improve cybersecurity awareness and digital safety for family members, including elderly people and less technically experienced users.

**Goals:**
- Help family members recognize common scams.
- Improve awareness of digital fraud.
- Access understandable security guidance.
- Support family members during suspicious incidents.

**Challenges:**
- Family members may not recognize impersonation attempts.
- Elderly users may trust fraudulent calls or messages.
- Limited understanding of online privacy and security.
- Difficulty responding correctly during emergencies.

**Expected Rakshak AI Features:**
- Family Protection.
- Message Scanner.
- Voice Fraud Intelligence.
- Emergency Response Center.
- Security Awareness Guidance.

**Success Scenario:**

A family member receives a suspicious call claiming to represent a bank. Rakshak AI provides warning signs and safe next steps without requiring sensitive banking information.

---

### Persona 3: Student and Young Internet User

**Persona ID:** RP-003  
**User Category:** Student  
**Technical Knowledge:** Basic to Intermediate

**Profile:**

A student or young internet user who frequently uses online learning platforms, social media, gaming services, digital payments and job portals.

**Goals:**
- Avoid fake internship and employment opportunities.
- Identify fraudulent websites and applications.
- Protect online accounts.
- Learn practical cybersecurity concepts.

**Challenges:**
- Fake job and internship offers.
- Fraudulent links shared through social media.
- Gaming account theft attempts.
- Limited awareness of phishing and identity theft.

**Expected Rakshak AI Features:**
- URL Scanner.
- Social Identity Intelligence.
- Password Safety Guidance.
- AI Cybersecurity Assistant.
- Personal Cyber Protection.

**Success Scenario:**

A student receives an internship offer requesting an advance payment. Rakshak AI helps identify suspicious indicators and explains how to verify the opportunity safely.

---

### Persona 4: Cybersecurity Analyst

**Persona ID:** RP-004  
**User Category:** Security Professional  
**Technical Knowledge:** Advanced

**Profile:**

A cybersecurity professional responsible for investigating suspicious activities, reviewing threat indicators and supporting security operations.

**Goals:**
- Investigate suspicious digital indicators.
- Review detection evidence.
- Correlate related threat information.
- Reduce repetitive investigation work.
- Maintain accurate incident records.

**Challenges:**
- Large volumes of suspicious indicators.
- Incomplete or outdated threat intelligence.
- False positives in automated detection.
- Time-consuming investigation workflows.

**Expected Rakshak AI Features:**
- Threat Intelligence Engine.
- Threat Correlation Engine.
- Risk Scoring Engine.
- Fraud Incident Manager.
- Analyst Investigation Dashboard.
- Audit Logs.

**Success Scenario:**

An analyst investigates multiple suspicious URLs associated with a possible scam campaign. Rakshak AI presents available evidence, related indicators and source freshness to support the investigation.

---

### Persona 5: Business and Organization

**Persona ID:** RP-005  
**User Category:** Enterprise  
**Technical Knowledge:** Intermediate to Advanced

**Profile:**

An organization that wants to improve its cybersecurity awareness, monitor reported threats and manage security incidents.

**Goals:**
- Improve organizational cyber safety.
- Manage security incidents centrally.
- Monitor relevant threat information.
- Maintain security records and reports.
- Control employee access to security tools.

**Challenges:**
- Employees falling for phishing attempts.
- Difficulty coordinating incident response.
- Lack of centralized visibility.
- Managing access and security responsibilities.

**Expected Rakshak AI Features:**
- Enterprise Security Dashboard.
- Team and Role Management.
- Incident Management.
- Security Reporting.
- Audit Trails.
- API Integrations.

**Success Scenario:**

An employee reports a suspicious email. The organization's authorized security team reviews the evidence, manages the incident and records the response through Rakshak AI.

---

### Persona 6: Platform Administrator

**Persona ID:** RP-006  
**User Category:** Platform Operations  
**Technical Knowledge:** Advanced

**Profile:**

An authorized administrator responsible for maintaining Rakshak AI's operational security, user access, configurations and platform health.

**Goals:**
- Maintain secure platform operations.
- Manage user roles and permissions.
- Monitor security events.
- Review administrative activities.
- Support incident recovery.

**Challenges:**
- Unauthorized access attempts.
- Misconfigured permissions.
- Security vulnerabilities.
- Operational failures and service disruptions.
- Maintaining reliable audit records.

**Expected Rakshak AI Features:**
- Administration Console.
- Identity and Access Security.
- API Defense.
- Security Monitoring.
- Vulnerability Management.
- Backup and Recovery.
- Audit Management.

**Success Scenario:**

An administrator identifies unusual authentication activity, investigates the relevant security logs and follows the approved response procedure.

## 4. Common User Expectations

Across all personas, Rakshak AI should aim to provide:

1. Clear and understandable security information.
2. Evidence-based detection results.
3. Privacy-conscious data handling.
4. Reliable and transparent recommendations.
5. Simple and accessible interfaces.
6. Appropriate control over user data.
7. Honest communication about detection limitations.

---

## 5. User Experience Principles

### 5.1 Simplicity

Users with limited technical knowledge should be able to understand and use essential security features.

### 5.2 Transparency

The platform should explain why something is considered suspicious and identify the evidence supporting its assessment.

### 5.3 Privacy

Users should not be required to disclose unnecessary personal or financial information.

### 5.4 Accessibility

Core security features should be usable across different devices and levels of technical knowledge.

### 5.5 User Control

Users should retain appropriate control over their data, permissions and security-related decisions.

---

## 6. Persona-Based Feature Prioritization

The initial MVP will primarily focus on:

- Individual digital users.
- Students and young internet users.
- Users seeking basic fraud assistance.

The first release should provide useful and functional URL scanning, message scanning, an AI cybersecurity assistant and basic incident management.

Family, analyst, enterprise and advanced administrator capabilities will be developed progressively.

---

## 7. Future Validation

These personas are initial product assumptions, not validated research findings.

Before production release, Rakshak AI should validate them through:

- User interviews.
- Usability testing.
- Feedback collection.
- Analysis of real-world cybersecurity needs.
- Testing with different technical skill levels.

The personas should be updated when research reveals new user needs or changes in usage patterns.
